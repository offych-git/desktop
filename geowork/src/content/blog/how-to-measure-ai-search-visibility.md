Your website traffic dashboard tells you a lot about visitors. It rarely tells you the full story of what an AI assistant said before someone decided to visit—or never visited at all.

A useful AI search visibility program begins with **repeatable questions** and **careful measurement**.

## Step 1: Choose buyer-focused prompts

Start with prompts based on real buying decisions, not only your company's name. For example:

- “Who provides [service] for [customer type] in [location]?”
- “What should I compare when choosing a [category] provider?”
- “Which companies specialize in [specific requirement]?”
- “What does [service] typically include?”

Group prompts by intent: discovery, comparison, validation, and selection.

Focus on a manageable initial set. Ten well-chosen prompts can be more useful than a large list of questions with no commercial relevance.

## Step 2: Establish a baseline

For each prompt, record the date, AI platform, exact wording, brands mentioned, citations shown, and how your brand was described.

A spreadsheet can be enough to begin. Consistency matters more than a flashy dashboard.

Use the same prompts in future checks, but recognize that results are probabilistic. Answers may vary with location, personalization, model updates, or repeated runs.

## Step 3: Compare competitors

Track a few genuine competitors. Are they cited because of their own websites, directories, industry publications, comparison pages, or useful expert resources?

Do not assume that copying a page will produce the same outcome. Instead, look for information gaps and opportunities to publish stronger original material.

## Step 4: Look beyond mentions

A brand mention is not always positive or useful. Track:

- **Presence:** Is the brand mentioned at all?
- **Accuracy:** Is the explanation of what the company does correct?
- **Context:** Does the mention appear for relevant, high-intent prompts?
- **Citations:** What sources support the answer?
- **Traffic:** Can relevant referral visits be identified in analytics?
- **Business outcomes:** Are qualified inquiries changing?

This is a more complete picture than a single Share of Voice percentage.

## Step 5: Review and prioritize

A recurring measurement rhythm lets you connect observed changes to specific work: new service pages, technical fixes, improved explanations, or independent mentions.

Keep the baseline visible and avoid attributing every change to one optimization. AI systems change continuously.

## Avoid these measurement traps

**One screenshot is not a trend.** Repeat checks and record methods.

**A citation is not a sale.** Treat visibility as an input to business performance.

**A prompt set is not the whole market.** Explain the scope of each report.
